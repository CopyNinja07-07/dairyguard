V1 · LOG ONLY–

Reads the temperature log and gives one answer for every carton on the truck, because every carton has the same log.

V2 · LOG + HEAT TEST–

Adds one heat-flow (calorimetry) test of each batch at dispatch: how fast its bacteria start to give off heat at 20 °C. Now each carton gets its own answer.

The heat test is simulated with a 5% measurement error. Across 48,000 simulated trips it recovers 69–94% of the accuracy the log alone cannot reach (paper v2, Results).

Fine print

What this does not tell you.

  • The trucks, fridges and heat-flow tests are simulated. Real delivery logs, calorimeter measurements on milk and new lab counts are the next step.
  • It estimates how much bacteria grew, not whether anyone gets sick. It is not a food-safety verdict: if milk smells or looks off, do not drink it.
  • About half of the simulated trips spoil by design, so the percentages are not real-world rates.
