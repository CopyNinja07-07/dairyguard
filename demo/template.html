Real growth records

From ComBase, a public database of growth experiments.

Natural spoilage flora in pasteurised milk (51 records) follow the same temperature shape, but grow about 1.6× slower than lab strains. Pick "Fitted to real milk flora" above: the conclusions hold.

Fine print

What this does not show.

  • Trucks and fridges are simulated. Strain spread and starting load are assumptions; real delivery logs and new lab counts are the next step.
  • This estimates bacterial growth, not illness. It is not a food-safety verdict. When in doubt, throw it out.
  • About half the simulated trips spoil by design, so the odds shown are not real-world rates.
